# ViralClip Personal: Privacy Policy
ViralClip Personal is a private tool used only by its owner on their own computer.
- It signs in to the owner's own TikTok account with TikTok's official Login Kit.
- It reads the owner's own videos and their view, like, comment and share counts, and uploads clips the owner approves to the owner's TikTok drafts.
- The sign-in token and data stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
- The owner can disconnect at any time in ViralClip or in TikTok's settings, which deletes the token.
